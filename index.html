Mission

We are a safe, low-barrier place where people experiencing homelessness and those in need can access resources, rest, rejuvenate, and replenish.

Vision

An improved quality of life and hope for those in need.

What guides us

Our values

Honor our shared humanity

We approach every interaction with respect, compassion, and empathy.

Reduce barriers

We serve others unconditionally, working to remove obstacles and support independent decision making.

Reach for excellence

We stay flexible and adaptable, striving to provide quality resources and services.

On site

What we offer

  • Breakfast & a hot lunch — every open day
  • Free clothing & a "store" — toiletries, hats, gloves, mittens
  • Survival gear — sleeping bags and tents
  • Showers — often the only access our guests have
  • Wednesday partner services — case management, health care, recovery, and more
  • Health on Wheels — mobile medical unit, multiple times a month
