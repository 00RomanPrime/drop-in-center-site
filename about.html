Our story

Built by volunteers, one open door at a time.

The Drop-In Center opened in February 2022 and has since served more than 1,600 unique guests in the Auburn/Lewiston area. We're open just two days a week, but currently help around 250 people every week.

How we started

We were exclusively volunteer-operated until July 2025, when we hired a part-time Operations Manager to help facilitate the program's continued growth. Today we have a team of about 70 dedicated volunteers — and we always welcome more.

In February 2025, we moved from our original home at the First Universalist Church of Auburn (169 Pleasant Street) to Auburn's Community Resource Center at 121 Mill Street. Until our own kitchen facilities are completed, our friends at Bates College and Davinci's Restaurant have graciously provided regular meals, alongside community groups like John F. Murphy Homes and our own volunteers.

What a visit looks like

  1. Come as you are. No barriers, no requirements — just show up during open hours.
  2. Eat, wash up, and restock. Breakfast, a hot lunch, showers, and free clothing and gear from our store.
  3. Connect with a partner, if you'd like. On Wednesdays, community partners are on-site for case management, health care, recovery, and more.
On Wednesdays

Partner services, under one roof

Community partners join us to serve guests directly in case management, health care, harm reduction, mental health, recovery, employment, sexual assault prevention and support, Veterans services, and housing navigation. We also host the Health on Wheels mobile medical unit multiple times a month.

Stewardship

Running lean, on purpose

Our annual operating costs are approximately $100,000 a year — modest by design, and made possible by donors, in-kind gifts, and a volunteer team that shows up week after week. Every gift, whether it's a shift, a bag of socks, or a check, goes directly toward keeping the door open.
