In-kind giving

Donate new or gently used items

These go straight into our free store, for guests to take as needed.

  • Adult clothing and outerwear (all sizes, all genders)
  • Socks and underwear (all sizes, all genders)
  • Gloves, mittens, hats
  • Adult shoes (all sizes, all genders)
  • Sleeping bags, blankets, tents & tarps
  • Flashlights, reusable water bottles
  • Backpacks of any kind
  • Body wipes, body powder, bug repellent
  • Individually packaged snacks
  • Canned goods that don't require a can opener

Drop-off details

Bring items to 121 Mill Street during our open hours — Wednesdays and Saturdays, 9am–1pm.

Drive down the left side of the building to the glass door at the top of the ramp.
